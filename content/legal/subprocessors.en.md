# Sub-processor list

This maintained list sets out the **sub-processors** that {{titolare.ragione_sociale}} relies on to deliver the appgrove
service. It is referenced by the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md) and by data processing agreements (DPAs).

> **The Italian version prevails.** In case of any discrepancy between language versions, the Italian version governs.

## Current sub-processors

| Provider | Purpose | Region | Data categories |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** | Hosting, database, storage and email delivery | European Union (eu-west-1, eu-central-1) | Account data, application usage data, technical logs |
| **Plausible Analytics** | Marketing-site statistics, cookieless | European Union (Hetzner, Germany) | Aggregate, anonymous site usage data |

Data is at rest in the European Union. AWS has a US parent company: any transfers are covered by the **EU-US Data Privacy
Framework** and **Standard Contractual Clauses**, with encryption.

## Paddle (Merchant of Record — not a sub-processor)

Payments are handled by **Paddle** as **Merchant of Record**: Paddle acts as an **independent controller** for payment
and tax data, not as a sub-processor. It therefore does not appear in the table above. See the "Payments" section of the
[Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md).

## Changes to the list

When we add or replace a sub-processor, we update this list and **notify users with 30 days' notice**, to allow any
objections. For information: **{{titolare.email_privacy}}**.
