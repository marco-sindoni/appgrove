# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how **{{titolare.ragione_sociale}}** ({{titolare.forma}}), established at {{titolare.sede}},
VAT {{titolare.piva}} (the "Controller", "we") processes the personal data of users of the appgrove service, available
at {{titolare.dominio}}, pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

> **The Italian version prevails.** In case of any discrepancy between language versions of this document, the Italian
> version governs.

## 1. Data controller

The data controller is {{titolare.ragione_sociale}}, {{titolare.sede}}. For any request concerning your personal data or
the exercise of your rights, write to **{{titolare.email_privacy}}**.

## 2. Data categories, purposes and legal bases

| Data category | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Account data (email, name, credentials) | Registration, authentication and service provision | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Application usage data | Providing the features of the subscribed applications | Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Payment and tax data | Subscription management, invoicing and tax duties | Legal obligation and contract — **processed by Paddle** as Merchant of Record (see §4) |
| Technical and security logs | Security, abuse prevention, diagnostics | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Marketing-site statistics (Plausible, cookieless) | Measuring site usage in aggregate | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — no consent required |
| Support and rights requests | Handling support and data-subject requests | Contract and legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(b)/(c)) |

We do not process special categories of data (Art. 9) and we do not carry out automated decision-making producing legal
effects on the data subject.

## 3. Recipients and processors

To deliver the service we rely on selected **sub-processors**, listed in the maintained
[sub-processor list](subprocessors.en.md). In short:

- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** — hosting, database, storage and email delivery, with data at rest in the European Union
  (regions eu-west-1 and eu-central-1);
- **Plausible Analytics** — marketing-site statistics, cookieless, with infrastructure in the European Union.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 4. Payments — Paddle Merchant of Record

Payments are handled by **Paddle**, acting as **Merchant of Record**: Paddle sells the subscription, collects payment,
issues the invoice and manages taxes, processing payment and tax data as an independent controller. Such processing is
governed by Paddle's own privacy notice. We do not store full card data.

## 5. International transfers

Data is processed and stored within the European Union. The only provider with a non-EU parent company is AWS: any
transfers are covered by the **EU-US Data Privacy Framework** and **Standard Contractual Clauses**, with encryption.
Plausible is an EU company with EU infrastructure (no transfer).

## 6. Retention periods

| Category | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account data | For the duration of the relationship; deletion or anonymisation after closure, save legal obligations |
| Application usage data | According to the retention declared by each application |
| Technical and security logs | From 7 days to 12 months depending on the log type |
| Payment and tax data | Retained by Paddle as required by law (not retained by us) |
| Support and rights requests | Up to 24 months from closure of the request |

## 7. Your rights

You may at any time exercise the rights of **access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection**
(Arts. 15-22 GDPR) by writing to **{{titolare.email_privacy}}**. Several of these actions are also available directly
from your account area. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Italy,
the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

## 8. Cookies

The service uses only essential technical cookies and cookieless statistics. Details are in the
[cookie notice](cookie.en.md); no consent banner is shown.

## 9. Minors

The service is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors without the consent of
whoever holds parental responsibility.

## 10. Changes

This policy is versioned (`version` and `effective_date` fields). For **material** changes you will be asked to accept
again; minor changes will be notified to you. The current version is always published at {{titolare.dominio}}.
