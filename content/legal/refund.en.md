# Refund Policy

This policy describes the refund conditions for subscriptions to the appgrove service, available at {{titolare.dominio}}.
It should be read together with the [Terms & Conditions](terms.en.md).

> **The Italian version prevails.** In case of any discrepancy between language versions, the Italian version governs.

## 1. Merchant of Record

Sales, payments and refunds are handled by **Paddle** as **Merchant of Record**. Refund requests are handled under this
policy and the **Paddle Buyer Terms**.

## 2. General rule

Except as required by law or in the cases below, purchases are treated as **final sales**: once the subscription is
activated and the service is made available, the fee is non-refundable for the period already started. Cancelling the
subscription prevents future renewals; the current period remains active until it expires.

## 3. Right of withdrawal (EU consumers)

If you are a consumer in the European Union, the **Paddle Buyer Terms** grant you a **14-day right of withdrawal** from
the purchase. By activating and using the digital service within that period with your consent, you may lose the right of
withdrawal for the part already consumed, within the limits of the law.

## 4. Exceptions

We will consider a refund, even beyond the above, in case of a **duplicate charge**, an unresolved **material
malfunction** or an **unauthorised charge**.

## 5. How to request a refund

Write to **{{titolare.email_support}}** stating your account email and the order reference, or use the Paddle references
in your purchase confirmation email. Mandatory rights granted by the applicable law in {{titolare.giurisdizione}} remain
unaffected.
