# Cookie Notice

The appgrove service ({{titolare.dominio}}) uses **only essential technical cookies** and **cookieless** statistics. For
this reason **no consent banner is shown**: we use no profiling or marketing cookies.

> **The Italian version prevails.** In case of any discrepancy between language versions, the Italian version governs.

## 1. Essential technical cookies

| Purpose | Description | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Authentication | Session/refresh token (HttpOnly) to keep you securely signed in | Session / token lifetime |
| Security | Optional anti-abuse token (protection against forged requests) | Session |
| Preferences | Essential technical settings (e.g. language) | Persistent, short |

These cookies are **necessary** for the Service to work and do not require consent under applicable law.

## 2. Cookieless statistics

On the marketing site we use **Plausible Analytics**, a **cookieless**, privacy-friendly solution that measures site
usage in **aggregate and anonymous** form, with infrastructure in the European Union. We do not track users across sites
and do not build individual profiles.

## 3. No third-party marketing cookies

We do not set advertising, social-network or third-party tracking cookies.

## 4. Managing cookies

Because we only use essential technical cookies, no consent choice is required. You may still manage or delete cookies in
your browser settings; disabling technical cookies may impair the Service. For information: **{{titolare.email_privacy}}**.
See also the [Privacy Policy](privacy.en.md).
